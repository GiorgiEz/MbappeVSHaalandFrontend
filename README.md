# Mbappé vs Haaland

A web app for comparing the career statistics of Kylian Mbappé and Erling Haaland — covering all-time, club, and country stats, along with individual awards and team trophies.

**🔗 Live Demo:** [mbappevshaaland.pages.dev](https://mbappevshaaland.pages.dev/)

## All-Time Stats

Career-wide statistics across every club and international appearance.

- **Career** — lifetime totals, split by club and country
- **Age** — stats grouped by player age
- **Competitions** — stats broken down by competition tier and individual competition
- **Favourite Opponents** — goals and appearances ranked by opponent
- **Finals** — performance in cup and tournament finals
- **Seasons** — season-by-season breakdown, split by club and country

## Club Stats

Statistics scoped to club football only.

- **Clubs** — stats for each club played for
- **Competitions** — club competitions broken down by tier
- **Seasons** — club stats by season

## Country Stats

Statistics scoped to international football only.

- **Competitions** — international competitions broken down by tier
- **Years** — stats grouped by calendar year

## Honours

Individual awards and team trophies won throughout each player's career.

- **Individual Awards** — Golden Boy, Golden Shoe, Player of the Year, and more
- **Team Trophies** — league titles, domestic and continental cups, and international trophies
